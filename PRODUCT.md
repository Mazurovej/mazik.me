# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Minecraft server owners, developers, and potential clients looking for high-quality Minecraft resources, plugins, and custom scripts.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a professional portfolio that presents Minecraft plugins and Skript systems like real, premium products rather than just portfolio cards.

## Brand Personality
Clean, modern developer style, slightly Minecraft-inspired (but not cringe), professional, premium.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS templates, overly bright/light themes, unstructured generic portfolio cards, clunky 2010s Minecraft server website aesthetics.

## Design Principles
- Present as a product: Each project is a polished product with stats and clear value.
- Tactical minimalism: Dark mode by default, restrained neon accents (Minecraft green).
- High density, high clarity: Bento-grid layouts to show stats and lists compactly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast for text against dark backgrounds, clear focus states for interactive elements, standard WCAG AA contrast for primary text.
